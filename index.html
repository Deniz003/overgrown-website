About Overgrown

Hey! Overgrown is an upcoming Roblox myth game focused on exploration, atmosphere and discovery. The goal is to create a calm and immersive world with unusual environments, industrial structures, hidden locations and plenty of things to discover.

Our Inspiration

This project will be moderately inspired by the game on Roblox: Ocean Terror. It's also a pretty cool game about myth and discovery.

Development

The project is still in its early stages, and there's quite a lot of work to be done. I'm currently looking for people who would like to help bring Overgrown to life. Whether you're interested in building or scripting, your contribution would be appreciated!

Development status: somehow still alive.
