About Overgrown

Overgrown is an upcoming Roblox myth game focused on exploration, atmosphere and discovery. The project aims to create a calm and immersive world featuring unusual environments, industrial structures, hidden locations and environmental storytelling.

Development

The project is currently in development. We are building a team of people who share an interest in creating atmospheric environments and memorable experiences.
