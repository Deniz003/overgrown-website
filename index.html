01 INTRODUCTION

WHAT IS OVERGROWN?

A place beyond
the familiar.

Overgrown is an upcoming Roblox myth game built around exploration, atmosphere and discovery.

Its world will combine unusual environments, hidden locations, quiet caves and industrial structures. The intention is to create somewhere that feels interesting to explore without needing to constantly demand your attention.

There is still a lot to create, and the project is only just beginning.

02 INFLUENCES

WHERE IT STARTED

Ideas from
other worlds.

One of the project's inspirations is Ocean Terror on Roblox. Its approach to myth, exploration and discovery helped influence the direction of Overgrown.

Overgrown will have its own identity, environments and ideas. Inspiration is a starting point, not a blueprint.

03 PROGRESS

CURRENTLY IN DEVELOPMENT

Still taking
shape.

Overgrown is in its early development stages. Environments, systems and the overall direction of the game are still being worked on.

I'm currently looking for people who would like to contribute to the project and help turn these ideas into something playable.

Development is ongoing
